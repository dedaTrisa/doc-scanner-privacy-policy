Doc Scanner - Image to PDF - 隐私政策 (中文)

应用信息

应用名称: Doc Scanner - Image to PDF（文档扫描仪）

开发者: Vladimir Gavranic

适用版本: 3.0.0 及以上

数据收集与使用

本应用将文档照片转换为 PDF 文件，全部在您的设备上完成。您的照片、页面和 PDF 文件仅在您的设备上处理和保存。本应用没有网络访问权限，没有账户，不使用统计分析或广告，不会向开发者或任何第三方传输或共享任何数据、图像或文档。

权限说明

相机权限：仅当您点击“TAKE PHOTO”或“LIVE CAMERA”拍摄文档时使用，首次使用时系统会请求您授权。本应用不申请存储权限，也不申请网络权限。

相册照片

点击“GALLERY”时会打开系统图片选择器，只有您选择的图片会提供给本应用。

保存与分享

点击“SAVE PDF”时，PDF 文件通过系统接口保存到设备的“下载（Download）”文件夹。正在编辑的页面保存在应用私有存储空间中，直到您删除。只有当您在“My scans”中点击“Share”时，PDF 才会交给您自己选择的应用（例如电子邮件），去向完全由您决定，开发者不会收到任何内容。本应用已关闭云备份。

数据主体权利

您可以随时在应用内删除页面（Del）、全部页面（Remove all pages）和已保存的扫描件（My scans → Delete），也可以通过设备文件管理器删除“下载”文件夹中的 PDF 文件。卸载应用会删除应用私有数据；“下载”文件夹中的 PDF 文件会保留，直到您自行删除。

联系方式

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 gavranic76@gmail.com 联系我们。

Doc Scanner - Image to PDF - Privacy Policy (English)

App Information

App name: Doc Scanner - Image to PDF

Developer: Vladimir Gavranic

Applies to: version 3.0.0 and later

Data Collection and Usage

The app turns photos of documents into PDF files, entirely on your device. Your photos, pages and PDF files are processed and stored only on your device. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. No data, image or document is transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions

Camera permission: used only when you tap "TAKE PHOTO" or "LIVE CAMERA" to photograph documents; Android asks for your permission the first time. The app requests no storage permission and no network permission.

Gallery Pictures

When you tap "GALLERY", the system picture picker opens and only the pictures you select are given to the app.

Saving and Sharing

When you tap "SAVE PDF", the PDF is saved to your device's Download folder through the system interface. Pages being edited are kept in the app's private storage until you delete them. Only when you tap "Share" in "My scans" is the PDF handed to the app you choose (for example e-mail); only you decide where it goes and the developer receives nothing. Cloud backup is switched off for this app.

Your Rights

You can delete pages ("Del"), all pages ("Remove all pages") and saved scans ("My scans" → "Delete") directly in the app at any time, or delete PDF files from the Download folder with your file manager. Uninstalling the app removes the app's private data; PDF files in the Download folder stay until you delete them.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at gavranic76@gmail.com.
