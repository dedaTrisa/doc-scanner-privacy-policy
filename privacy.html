Privacy Policy

All user data, captured document images, temporary files, and generated PDF documents are stored strictly locally on your device's internal storage and public Download folder. No data, image, or document is transmitted to external servers.

The app works entirely offline: all camera capture processing, image rotations, and PDF generations remain 100% on your device.

No data is shared with third parties.

Permissions Used

  • Camera Permission: Required solely to capture photos of physical documents directly within the app.
  • Storage Permission: Required solely to save the generated PDF documents into your device's Download folder and store temporary preview images locally.

Data Subject Rights

Users have full control over their data directly within the app and on their device:

  • Scanned temporary images can be deleted immediately using the "DELETE" button.
  • Saved PDF files can be viewed, managed, or deleted at any time through your device's File Manager in the Download folder.
  • Users may also delete all app-related temporary data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in their device settings.

隐私政策

所有用户数据、拍摄的文档图像、临时文件和生成的 PDF 文档均严格存储在您设备的本地内部存储和公共下载（Download）文件夹中。绝不会将任何数据、图像或文档传输到外部服务器。

本应用完全离线运行：所有相机拍摄处理、图像旋转和 PDF 生成均 100% 在您的设备上完成。

不会与第三方共享任何数据。

使用的权限

  • 相机权限： 仅用于在应用内直接拍摄实体文档照片。
  • 存储权限： 仅用于将生成的 PDF 文档保存到您设备的 Download 文件夹中，并在本地存储临时预览图像。

数据主体权利

用户可以在应用内和设备上直接完全控制他们的数据：

  • 使用“DELETE”按钮可以立即删除扫描的临时图像。
  • 保存的 PDF 文件随时可以通过设备“文件管理器”的 Download 文件夹进行查看、管理或删除。
  • 用户也可以通过卸载应用或在设备设置中清除应用存储数据来删除所有与应用相关的临时数据。
